| S21-P01 | Arbeláez et al. (2011), *Contour Detection and Hierarchical Image Segmentation*, DOI `10.1109/TPAMI.2010.161`; BIDS BSDS500 mirror commit `a04b7c6c3a9f0ace74bf205c72a43d32e1c72722`; Piotr Dollár `edges` commit `94260b5d0fc068202598312e01a37604972dcc9e` | Primary Berkeley ODS/OIS/AP: all annotations, one-to-one `correspondPixels`, 99 thresholds, `maxDist=0.0075`, thinning. | S21-P02 | Estrada & Jepson (2009), *Benchmarking Image Segmentation Algorithms*, DOI `10.1007/s11263-009-0251-z`; author manuscript `https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~jepson/papers/benchSegEstradaJepsonIJCV2009.pdf` | Defines directional proximity correspondence, no-intervening-contour and same-side constraints, and separate directional precision/recall. | S21-P03 | Amorim et al. Stage 21 uses development-allowed frozen maps and must not read BSDS test or treat this F as Berkeley ODS. |
| S21-P04 | Kermit Image Toolkit (KITT), `https://github.com/giaracvi/KITT`, commit `d70bb66ef9ab6f5cea8ead528fe5ac15ac096baf`, GPLv3 | Public implementation cited by the target fuzzy lineage. `oneToManyEJBC` unions machine matches across annotations for precision and accumulates annotation matches/counts for recall. | Evaluator utility | Hash-pinned ignored dependency. Public v1.01 matcher calls an absent helper beyond its one-pixel bootstrap; Stage 21A uses the byte-identical complete v1.00 backup shipped in the same commit, renamed only for MATLAB loading. This is best-public-source fidelity, not an exact private-paper-code claim. |

Stage 21 protocol entries do not promote a detector. Berkeley `0.025` and
Estrada–Jepson `0.025` are separate endpoints; neither can rescue failure at
the primary Berkeley `0.0075` endpoint.
