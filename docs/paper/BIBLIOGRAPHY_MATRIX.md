## Main literature-derived hypotheses we are testing

1. **Context-conditioned nonadditivity:** image heterogeneity should alter the capacity and possibly the aggregation operator itself.
2. **Selection before fusion:** Shapley/context gates may improve generalization by removing locally irrelevant/redundant cues before Choquet fusion.
3. **Hierarchical nonadditivity:** descriptor interaction and scale interaction are different phenomena and should be modeled at different levels.
4. **Localization/context separation:** MFI should refine/control a precise localizer rather than simply be added to the localizer score.
5. **Compact capacities may generalize better:** distorted-probability and regularized interaction families may be more stable than fully free capacities on small datasets.
6. **RDFs may improve robustness:** d-CF/d-XC/d-CC should be tested under blur/noise/texture rather than only on clean data.
7. **Uncertainty is a signal, not an error term:** scale disagreement and annotation disagreement can control thresholds, linking, and training weights.
8. **Granularity should be explicit:** structural contours and fine texture edges are different targets; one deterministic map may be unnecessarily restrictive.
9. **Fuzzy fusion may be strongest at the detector level:** future MFI-Hybrid experiments should fuse MFI, TEED/PiDiNet and classical localizers using context-dependent capacities.
